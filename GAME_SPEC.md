# Implemented rules, schema 3

Setting: original human mercenary company in the fractured Marches. No supernatural units or spells. Strategic coordinates shown one-based; stored sector IDs are y×16+x.

Campaign.s stores 256 sectors, stable mercenary/squad/group/job IDs, factions, stash, transaction ledger, timestamped events, PRNG state and encounter/battle state. Squad packs and sector depots own food, medicine and tools; the global supply fields are removed. Units are dictionaries; scene nodes never own gameplay values. Each item has a unique ID, catalogue kind, quantity and condition. Equipment references owned IDs. Enemy profiles belong to group fighter rosters; levy profiles belong to their home sector. Combat/UI derive loadout values through catalog accessors.

The Ashford stockpile has a strategic location. Taking, depositing and buying there require a stationary local squad. Transfers between fighters require the same sector; in combat the source must be active, the recipient adjacent, and the handover costs 3 AP. Equipped gear must be unequipped first. Switching gear keeps the previous item in the backpack; all carried gear adds encumbrance. Individual weapons, shields, armour and helmets wear through attacks, blocks, parries or absorbed damage, and repairs target an actual damaged item. Ammunition is an owned stack whose quantity decreases when fired.

## Clock and economy

Time is integer minutes, initially day 1 08:00. Events sort by timestamp then stable event ID. Arrival/attack interrupts time at contact. Each hour consumes one food per living hired mercenary from their own squad and one per living uncaptured levy from the home depot. Shortage affects only that store's people: −5 morale and +8 fatigue. Split divides each supply by member proportion with integer remainders retained by the source; merge sums exact stores. Transfers require positive whole quantities, stationary co-location and company control for depot access. Exchanges are blocked during an encounter. Market purchases fill the selected squad's pack. Farms produce 50×loyalty×order/10,000 food into the local depot; mines produce 100×that factor crowns. Midnight charges mercenary wages plus 3 per living levy. Debt remains accounted and reduces company morale.

Recruitment costs the profile fee; wages continue separately. Seven-day employment expires through scheduled events. Renewal costs seven wages and extends seven days (an additional renewal fee, not prepaid wages). Seven initial five-day postings implement capture (two), named-force elimination, occupied camp assault, caravan escort, ordered patrol and settlement defence. Payment is idempotent and requires a real arrival or victorious battle before the exclusive deadline. Acceptance is blocked during contact. Captures require uncontrolled territory; named forces must remain capable; defence requires company ownership. Patrol/escort bind to a capable stationary squad at Ashford. Patrol visits [33,49,81,17] in order; return is required. Escort owns 20 freight food outside the provision pack, adds 2 weight, and delivers it to sector 49 only after safe arrival or victorious ambush resolution. Withdrawal/defeat/disbanding loses cargo. Defence requires the named group's actual defeat at the target settlement; off-site destruction fails that defence objective. An unmet deadline, settlement loss, lost assigned squad or target removed without the contracted victory fails work. Escort/patrol can repost after one day, with unique IDs and a new deadline event. No manual success control exists. A caravan is currently abstract strategic freight following its squad, without an independent tactical cart/driver.

Travel uses four-neighbour weighted shortest paths. Road/grass/forest/hills/marsh base minutes: 45/70/100/115/140. The slowest member's fatigue, injury and inventory burden set a group multiplier. Pack weight (food×0.1 + medicine×0.2 + tools) divided by member count adds weight/60 to that multiplier; food shortage then adds 30%. This is a soft burden model, not a hard supply-capacity limit. Combat AP uses personal inventory rather than the shared provision pack. ETA is an estimate: each leg recomputes current conditions. Foot transport only.

Medical care consumes one medicine from the patient's squad and heals 2+best capable stationary local caregiver skill/15, stops bleeding and reduces wounds. Remote doctors and remote stores confer no benefit. Local levies can help; an isolated unconscious person cannot self-treat. Levy bleeding treatment similarly uses the home depot and capable local care. Repairs consume the worker's own squad tools and use smithing; workshops add four repair points. Personal training yields 1+wisdom/40 points/hour, plus two at training grounds; 12 points adds one melee skill, capped at 95. Rest heals one HP/hour when not bleeding. Guard raises local order; scout reveals neighbouring sectors.

Recruiting a levy costs 80 crowns and creates a persistent original spear/padded-armour/shield profile, with no free rations. Maximum 12 living levies per sector for new recruitment. Garrison training advances by 1+leadership/25 per assignment hour, plus three per day for an active garrison. Each 20-point level adds two melee and one ranged skill to existing living uncaptured defenders. New recruits inherit the current level. Levies use their actual equipment, HP, AP, morale, fatigue and wounds in tactical defence.

## Tactical rules

New contacts begin in a deployment phase. Default friendly positions are reviewed before combat. Placement is restricted to unblocked tiles in the battlefield's authored deployment rectangles (the western six columns for older fields), unoccupied except by the selected fighter and at least four Chebyshev tiles from every living enemy. Placement and facing (eight directions) cost no AP; actual profiles, resources, PRNG and strategic clock do not advance. Enemies are placed from remembered positions first; incoming friendlies never displace them. If the zone cannot fit everyone, the excess units remain unplaced, confirmation rejects, and the player can save or use the existing retreat rules. Company squads, combined local squads and home levies share the formation. Begin combat validates every living friendly, preserves initial AP and starts company round 1 without a bleeding tick. AI, attacks (including reactions), movement, equipment changes and knowledge refresh are blocked before confirmation. Confirmation is irreversible for that encounter. Unconfirmed phase/positions/facing/placement flags persist; active saves without a phase resume combat in place.

New fields are original deterministic 18×14 authored archetypes: settlement (market/workshop/garrison/training facilities), farm, mine and hostile camp. Wilderness uses road, forest, hills, marsh or grass variations. Settlement roads cost 1 AP per orthogonal step before actor modifiers; crops cost 4 and conceal 25; quarry costs 3 and is elevated; ore/stalls/tents supply cover. Buildings have wall footprints, walkable floor and openable doors, with no roofs or interior-mode transitions. Farm retains the original yard-wall/door relationships, with added crop rows. Mine uses a five-column entry; camp splits its entry into two rectangles. Other authored zones are stored explicitly. Every layout has a non-blocked hostile spawn pool; remembered hostile positions take precedence, and newcomers choose free authored spawns before a general fallback. Layout/version/zones/spawns are owned by sector.battlefield alongside tiles/ground and carried through active battles, saves and retreat. Existing fields without metadata keep their original geometry and legacy deployment strip; changing facility/ownership never regenerates a visited map.

18×14 encounter grids; logical grids support 160×160. Orthographic 3D, Compatibility renderer, MultiMesh floor, no per-tile physics. Wall, closed door and occupied destination block pathfinding; window blocks movement and passes sight. Diagonal corner cutting is rejected. Base movement 2 AP; hill costs 3, brush 4; rising one level adds 2; diagonal multiplies by 1.4 rounded up. Crouch adds 1, prone adds 3, run subtracts 1 (minimum 1), cautious adds 1. Preview and execution share step_cost.

Original AP formula: floor((10+agility/12+dexterity/24+experience×0.6) × (0.45+0.55×HP/maxHP) × (1−fatigue/180) × burden multiplier). Clamp 4..26; HP≤10 gives zero. Low morale reduces AP by 30%. Strength affects carrying capacity and melee damage; endurance affects travel/run exertion. Leadership supports daily morale and levy training. All listed base attributes have a simulation use.

Team turns refill AP without carryover. Bleeding ticks at own-team turn start and hourly outside combat. Unconscious units at HP≤10 cannot act; HP=0 is death. Player turn → enemy actions → next player turn; outcome derives from capable survivors. Every stationary friendly squad in the encounter sector and its company-owned levy roster deploy on the same side, without occupied/blocked placement. Garrison-only attacks create an engagement without teleporting remote mercenaries. Levies are player-controlled through normal tactical controls.

Retreat requires a capable mercenary and a safe adjacent sector. Each participating squad prefers its actual arrival sector, gains 10 fatigue and retains wounds. Levies remain at home; surviving defenders get a follow-on engagement after the company withdraws. If all friendly tactical units are incapacitated, living survivors are captured and cannot be rehired. An emptied defeated squad's provisions stay in the contested sector depot. Without capable home defenders, control passes to the actual attacking faction. Prisoner interactions beyond the captured state are not implemented.

Each visited battlefield retains tiles, doors and ground placements. Enemy profiles preserve wounds, HP, ammunition, equipment, reload state and positions through retreat/save/re-entry. Group and garrison strength derive from capable uncaptured people. Strategic group movement clears local positions, preserving people/gear. Death drops actual owned equipment; looting transfers that same item. Victory collects remaining loot into surviving mercenary backpacks. A garrison-only victory leaves uncollected loot on site for later company recovery. Contacts queue by sector, and stale route callbacks are rejected.

Hit percentage is clamp(50+skill×0.6−target agility×0.25+weapon accuracy+aim×7−fatigue/5−lost HP/5+morale modifier+experience×2+elevation×5+position modifiers,5,95). Ranged attacks additionally apply range, cover, concealment and stance penalties. Rear melee adds 22; nearby additional attackers add pressure. The displayed probability is the initial contact roll; eligible AP-funded block/parry/dodge can negate a successful contact. Bow reload costs 2 AP; crossbow reload costs 7. Ammunition is shared by missile category at present.

Armour reduces original damage by damage type; random body location changes head protection and damage. Cutting/piercing/projectiles cause persistent bleeding. Weapon durability affects damage and can be repaired. Medical action costs 5 AP and one medicine. Defence costs 2 AP and requires consciousness, morale and an eligible facing. Guard reserves current AP for melee disengagement or visible ranged reactions.

Visibility uses perception, facing, stance, concealment, walls/windows and elevation. Known opponents are distinct from actual units; AI can use remembered positions and sounds. Full unexplored terrain fog and lighting are outstanding. Current AI is a bounded patrol/investigate/approach/fire/reload/retreat policy, not JA2's full tactical decision model.

## Persistence and input

JSON version 3, integer PRNG, temporary write/flush, previous-generation .bak then rename. Load rebinds mercenaries, enemies, levies and active battlefield arrays to canonical campaign owners. Detached schema-1→2→3 migration leaves the original file untouched until saving. Schema-2 global supply pools divide proportionally among staffed squads, with remainders retained deterministically; if none are staffed, the base depot receives the pool. Old garrison counters become individual trained levy profiles, without creating supplies. Actual previous-release strategic and active-battle fixtures verify both migrations. Validation rejects duplicated items or squad memberships, negative/fractional supply quantities, reused item/levy IDs, inconsistent tactical profiles and malformed terrain. Unsupported versions and broader OS interruption tests remain open.

All essential implemented actions use on-screen controls. Tap previews before movement/attack; drag pans; pinch and +/- zoom. Panels scroll and destructive dismissal/retreat ask confirmation. Application pause writes autosave. Hardware cutout handling and multitouch behaviour still need device QA.
